Burger

Description

By the end of this recipe you will have a tasty and memorable burger recipe to take anywhere with you. This recipe requires little preparation, but has an outcome of a tasty meal.

This recipe is for a succulent burger, with a rich beef patty, topped with tomatoes, ketchup and cheese. In a lovely sesame seeded bun.

Ingredients

  • Burger Buns
  • Burger Patty
  • Tomatoes
  • Cheese
  • Ketchup

Steps

  1. Fry the burger patty
  2. Cut tomato into the amount of slices you want
  3. Grate cheese or remove cheese from packet
  4. Cut burger bun down the middle
  5. Put burger patty ontop of lower burger bun
  6. Put cheese ontop of burger patty.
  7. Put tomato pieces ontop of cheese
  8. Apply ketchup to taste
