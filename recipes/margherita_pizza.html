Burger

Description

By the end of this recipe you will have a tasty and memorable pizza recipe to take anywhere with you. This recipe requires little preparation, but has an outcome of a tasty meal.

This recipe is for a traditional neapolitan style pizza with a thin, chewy crust, topped with just tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella and basil. The result has a crisp, blistered edge, gooey melted cheese and bright herby freshness from the basil.

Ingredients

  • Bread Flour
  • Warm Water
  • Instant Yeast
  • Salt
  • Olive Oil
  • Canned tomatoes
  • Clove of garlic
  • Fresh mozzarella, in pieces
  • Basil leaves
  • Extra virgin olive oil
  • Salt to taste

Steps

  1. Combine flour, water, yeast and salt in a bowl.
  2. Mix until a shaggy dough forms
  3. Knead for 8-10 minutes until smooth and elastic, add olive oil half way through.
  4. PLace dough in an oiled bowl, cover and let rise for 1-2 hours until doubled.
  5. Divide into 2 balls, shape and let rest for 20-30 minutes.
  6. Preaheat oven to its highest setting, atleast 250 celcius
  7. Stretch dough intoo a round, leaving a thicker edge for the crust.
  8. Spread crushed tomatoes thinly, leaving border bare.
  9. Top with torn mozzarella pieces.
  10. Bake 8-12 minutes until the curst is blistered and golden.
  11. Remove, top with fresh basil leaves, and a drizzle of olive oil.
